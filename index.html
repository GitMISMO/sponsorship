Start Here: Home, the Outreach Planner, Sponsors and Prospects
  • The portal works like Member 360. Home is the day and the season at a glance, with Just Came In across the top.
  • Outreach Planner (it was To Do) works like Member 360’s. The dark band says where to start and counts what you communicated this week, follow-ups due, what is past due, what is planned and the applications waiting for review; select a number to go to it. Choose Everyone or one person, and This Week, Next Week or Past Due.
  • Up Next is the oldest thing past due, then today’s: Email them, Log a Touchpoint, Move to… another day or Mark Done. Show the next one skips ahead.
  • The week board has a column for each weekday (and Past Due when something was missed). Each card is a prospect’s follow-up date, a next step from a touchpoint, or a planned touch. Drag a card onto another day to move it (a prospect’s follow-up date moves with it), tick it when it’s done, or select it to open the company. Undo takes back the last change.
  • Who to Touch suggests sponsors and prospects by reason: prospects with no next date, prospects gone quiet, current sponsors not in for the next summit, benefits still to use, sponsors gone quiet, and past sponsors to win back. Drag a name onto a day, or use Plan for…. Anyone touched in the last week or already on the plan drops off.
  • Also Waiting holds applications to review, payments past due, invoices Bernie still has to raise, the mismosponsor@ emails and new prospects. All Follow-Ups at the bottom lists every follow-up, open or done, by owner.
  • Sponsors is Member 360’s Members page for sponsors, sponsors only: the tiles and tabs (Current, Applied, Past, All) choose who is listed. The dark band shows current sponsors by tier, what is outstanding for each summit, workgroups and each other kind, and Sponsors by Summit; select any of them to list just those companies. Filter by summit or kind, stage and owner, tick Outstanding Only, or jump with the A to Z bar. Prospects have their own page, Prospects.
  • Select any company to open its record, laid out like a Member 360 record: tier, sponsor since, when the sponsorship ends and this season’s status across the top; each deal on its stage bar; Money, Benefits Used and Open Deal; Key Contacts; Recent Activity; and Company Details. Log a Touchpoint, email them, or add a prospective sponsorship from there. Download Logo saves the logo the sponsor sent with the online prospectus, straight from the portal (no SharePoint sign-in). + Add a Logo keeps a logo a sponsor emailed (up to 4 MB), and Replace Logo swaps it. A sponsor already on the website gets Open the Logo. The file is also under Company Details and on the application.
  • New Sponsors on Sponsors lists every sponsorship that started in a period: This Month, Last Month, This Year, any month from Pick a Month, or your own Dates, for Summit, Workgroup, Other or all of them, with the total.
  • The Pipeline shows one kind at a time: Summit (where it opens), Workgroup or Other. Every number and stage box lists what makes it up.
  • Prospects is the same page as Sponsors, for prospects: Open, On Hold and Lost across the top; open prospects by stage, the forecast and prospects by summit (select any to see them); New Prospects for this month, last month, this year, any month or any dates; then the filters (what they are a prospect for, stage, owner, My Prospects Only, remembered) and one row per prospect. Select a row to open its company, where you set the stage and Which Summit.
  • Summit Sponsorships, Workgroup Sponsorships and Other Sponsorships are the full lists of each kind.
  • One company card. Wherever you select a sponsorship or a prospect (these pages, the Pipeline, Home), it opens inside its company’s card, under ← Back to the company, the way Member 360 does. The card itself shows everything the company has: every sponsorship, prospect and conversation.
How It All Fits Together

Five pieces work together. None of them needs anyone to copy information from one to another by hand.

  • The prospectus (resources.mismo.org/Sponsorship-Prospectus) is the public page. Sponsors build a sponsorship, sign the contract and submit there.
  • This portal is where the team works every sponsorship from application to payment. Only people with Sponsorship access can open it.
  • The Sponsorship Requests list in SharePoint (MISMO Summits site) holds one row per sponsorship. The prospectus reads it to show sponsors what is already sold.
  • The Sponsorship Team Emails flow in Power Automate sends the portal’s emails, keeps the Sponsorship Requests list in step with the portal, and copies booked sponsors to Summit HQ’s People tab.
  • MISMO Money (the Financial System) tells the portal which invoices Aptify shows as paid.

The portal’s records are kept in MISMO’s private data store. Every save is shared with everyone else using the portal within about 20 seconds.

The Sponsorship Process, Step by Step
  1. Submitted. A sponsor applies on the prospectus, or someone adds a New Application here. Amy and Kelly review it and set the Amount Due.
  2. Approved. Amy or Kelly clicks Approved. That emails Bernie the invoice request, and the opportunity comes off the public page. The logo goes onto the website automatically, so Vanessa is no longer emailed a logo request.
  3. Pending Payment. Bernie raises the invoice in Aptify and enters the Aptify order ID, the invoice date, the comp code and the number of comps on his Registration Desk in Summit HQ. The portal picks them up on its own (whenever it is open, and every hour) and the order ID moves it to Pending Payment, the same as if it were typed here. When he emails the sponsor their code from the Desk, the “comp code sent” benefit ticks itself. The record’s history says From the Registration Desk. They can still be typed here too.
  4. Paid. When Aptify shows the invoice paid, MISMO Money tells the portal and the record is marked Paid on its own (see Payments from MISMO Money). It can also be marked Paid by hand on the record. Jonna, Amy and Kelly get the Payment Made email.

In Kind is a sponsor who is recognized but never charged. Set Sponsorship Type to In Kind on the record. Approving it skips invoicing and payment altogether and moves it straight to In Kind: Bernie gets no invoice request, nothing is ever chased, and none of it reaches Committed, Collected, Outstanding or Overdue. It takes no opportunity off the list either — Inventory and the public prospectus carry on offering it, because a free sponsorship is recognition rather than a slot someone has bought. Its logo goes onto the website automatically, the same as a paid sponsor’s. The day after the last summit it covers, the company goes back on the Prospects list, noted as a prior in-kind sponsor, so the team can try to convert them to a paid sponsorship.

Declined closes a request that won’t go ahead. Written Off is a sponsorship that went ahead but was never paid. It keeps the slot taken and counts as committed, but not as collected.

When a Sponsor Applies on the Prospectus
  • The sponsor gets a reference number on screen (like SP-20260918-K3QT). The same number is saved on the record as its Prospectus Reference.
  • A row is added to the Sponsorship Requests list with status New, and the sponsor’s logo is saved to the Sponsor Logos library on the MISMO Summits site.
  • The application appears here under Submitted the next time anyone on the team opens the portal, with a message saying it came from the prospectus.
  • If the company is already a prospect, the two are linked, and the application shows everything recorded while it was a prospect.
  • The day the sponsor picked (for breakfasts, breaks and receptions) fills in the record’s Day field.
Payments from MISMO Money

MISMO Money loads Aptify payments each morning, from the daily payments email, and whenever someone adds payment files to it. Each time, it gives the portal a list of paid sponsorship orders. It matches on the Aptify order ID, which this portal holds as the invoice number.

The next time anyone who can edit opens the portal, every payment that changes something is recorded automatically:

  • a matched order still awaiting payment is marked Paid, with the amount received and the date paid;
  • a refund on an order already marked Paid corrects the Amount Paid;
  • where Aptify bills a different company name (Innovis for Factual Data, for example), it is filled in as Also Invoiced As.

A notice at the top of the portal lists what was recorded. Jonna, Amy and Kelly get the usual Payment Made email for each record marked Paid, and each record’s history says it came from MISMO Money.

Sponsorship payments that match no record here get their own notice. Click See Them to look. It usually means an order ID is missing from a record, or a sponsor was billed under another company name. An order is left off the notice when a record for the same company and the same summit or series is already Paid.

The foot of the left panel shows Payments updated with the date and time MISMO Money last sent payment information. Point at it to see the latest payment date it covers. It turns amber if it’s more than three days old, since MISMO Money normally updates every weekday morning.

It never marks anything unpaid. A payment missing from MISMO Money is not evidence that it wasn’t paid.

Chasing Payment
  • Payment is chased 30 days after the invoice, then every 10 business days until it clears. The aim is to collect before the summit.
  • When a chase is due, the record’s card on the Pipeline turns red and says so.
  • Send Alert emails the payment follow-up to Bernie, Jonna, Amy and Kelly. The button says Sending…, then a message confirms it went. Clicking it again within two minutes does nothing, so it can’t go out twice.
  • Sending the alert is not the chase. Once Bernie has actually chased the sponsor, he logs the follow-up on his Registration Desk (or Log a Follow-Up on the record here). Either records the date and starts the 10-business-day count to the next one.
  • If the count is ever wrong, correct Follow-Ups Logged and Last Follow-Up in the record’s Payment section and click Save.
The Automatic Emails
  • Invoice Request: to Bernie, Jonna copied. Sent when a sponsorship is approved.
  • Logo Request: to Vanessa, Jonna, Amy and Kelly copied, with a link to the logo in Sponsor Logos when the sponsor uploaded one. No longer sent on approval (the logo now goes onto the website automatically); it only goes if someone presses Logo Request on a record.
  • Payment Made: to Jonna, Amy and Kelly. Sent when a record is marked Paid, by hand or from MISMO Money.
  • Payment Follow-Up: to Bernie, Jonna, Amy and Kelly. Sent when someone clicks Send Alert on a chase.
  • List Update: no email. It updates the Sponsorship Requests list.

They usually go out within a minute. Each record’s Alerts section lists every one, with “on its way” or “emailed” and the date. The buttons there send one again.

The Public Page and the Sponsorship Requests List
  • The prospectus counts a slot as sold when its row on the list is Approved, Pending Payment or Paid. Sponsors see the change the next time they load the page.
  • An In Kind sponsorship reaches the list as New, which the prospectus does not count as sold, so its opportunity stays on offer. A free sponsorship takes nothing off the list.
  • The portal updates the list on its own whenever a sponsorship’s status, tier, opportunity or day changes. This only happens for sponsorships with a summit still to come, so editing an old record never changes what sponsors see.
  • A sponsorship entered straight into the portal gets its row on its first update.
  • The List Update button in a record’s Alerts section sends it to the list again by hand.
Days, Workshops and Custom Packages
  • Day. Receptions (Monday or Tuesday), breakfasts and both breaks (Monday to Thursday) are one sponsor a day. Pick the day under Opportunity. It shows on lists, emails, Inventory and the public page.
  • Workshop Name. Any workshop sponsorship has a field for the workshop’s name.
  • Custom package. For a Series deal off the price list, set Package to Custom. Then give Winter, Spring and Fall each a tier, an opportunity (any one still open at that summit) and a share of the Amount Due.
Workgroup Sponsorships
  • A company sponsoring one MISMO group for a term. The first one is the Initial; each cycle after it is a numbered Renewal. Cadence is Annual or Quarterly.
  • None of it touches the summits. No prospectus, no event, no tier, no inventory. It is its own revenue line and its own tab.
  • Draft means no invoice is out yet. Entering the Aptify order ID moves it to Pending Payment by itself, exactly like a summit sponsorship.
  • Payment arrives on its own. MISMO Money matches on the Aptify order ID, which these records hold as the invoice number, and marks the record Paid. No one watches for it by hand.
  • The invoice email is drafted, never sent. The record shows the message in full — recipients, subject, body. Read it, change anything, then press Send. Nothing leaves until you do.
  • Raise the Next Renewal On is the date the following cycle should be invoiced, carried over from the tracker.
  • Archived sponsors are hidden until you tick Show Archived, and the figures follow whatever is on screen.
Home, Sponsors and Prospects: It’s About the Company
  • Home is the day at a glance: what just came in, a line on the season (past due and follow-ups due open the Pipeline), the summit season’s money, the sales pipeline, the next summit, emails to mismosponsor@, and money by type. Select any tile to go to it.
  • Sponsors lists every company that sponsors now, has applied, or has sponsored before, once each: every sponsorship it has, the stage it’s at, its owner, its money over the last 12 months and its last touch. The tiles and the type buttons (Summit, Workgroup, each other type) filter the list.
  • Prospects lists every prospect, one row each: its type, what they’re interested in, the stage (change it right in the row), the owner, the forecast, the next follow-up and the last touch. One click on Summit and My Prospects shows just your summit prospects, and the page remembers that choice in your browser. The tiles count each stage; select one to see only it. Hold and Lost show the rest. The red count on the menu is your prospects due for a follow-up.
  • The company record brings everything together: its sponsorships of every kind and its prospects, its year-by-year sponsorship journey, money, contacts, and one Activity timeline of emails, notes and changes. Change a prospect’s stage on its stage bar, Log a Touchpoint (a call, email, meeting or conversation, what it was about, and a next step with a follow-up date), set the company’s Owner, or Add a Prospective Sponsorship. The buttons on each sponsorship open the record itself.
  • A row on the Pipeline opens its company too, and emails to mismosponsor@ are logged on the company, whatever sponsorship they’re about.
The Pipeline: Every Sponsorship, Lead to Paid
  • One list for everything: summit sponsorships, workgroup sponsorships and every Other Sponsorship type, prospects included. Each row shows its Type, what it is, its Stage, owner, amount, the next step and the last time anyone was in touch.
  • The stages: Lead, Contacted, Meeting Scheduled, Proposal, Verbal Commitment, Application In, Approved, Pending Payment and Paid, plus Hold and Lost. A prospect moves through the first five; you change its stage on its company card (the Stage box, or select a step on its bar). From Application In on, the sponsorship record carries the stage itself (a summit application, a workgroup or an other sponsorship), so it moves when that record does.
  • The band at the top has two rows: Prospects (Lead to Verbal Commitment, before they apply) and Sponsorships Being Closed (Application In, Approved and Pending Payment). Each box counts a stage and its money. Select any number there, or any stage, and the list of what makes it up opens right under it.
  • The Pipeline shows one kind at a time, Summit first, then Workgroup or Other; the buttons above the list narrow it by All Open, Prospects, Being Closed, Won, Hold, Lost or All. Won and Lost keep a year of history; All shows every record however old, archived ones included.
  • New Sponsorship… next to the search starts a summit application, a workgroup sponsorship or one of any other type, or adds A New Sponsorship Type.
  • New Prospects lists who was added this week or last. Due Now lists follow-ups that have come due, invoices past due, and applications or sponsorships waiting on a next step (review, raising the invoice, a payment chase). Each shows four; See All opens the rest.
  • A prospect for any kind of sponsorship: set its Type of Sponsorship (Summit, Workgroup or an Other type). A workgroup prospect names the MISMO group. When a workgroup or other prospect says yes, Start the Sponsorship on it opens the new record already filled in; save it and the prospect is marked Converted to Sponsorship. A summit prospect converts when its application comes in.
  • Give every prospect a Next Follow-Up date; it shows under Due Now when that day comes.
  • A prospect who applies comes off the list while the sponsorship runs. The day after it ends, it comes back as a Lead to renew, with every note kept. Every sponsor gets a prospect record this way, so past sponsors show up to renew. Delete Prospect removes one for good, and the portal won’t add it back on its own.
  • An In Kind sponsor comes back the same way, with a note saying they were recognized at no charge and are worth approaching for a paid sponsorship. They count as Retention, like any other returning sponsor.
  • Summit, Workgroup and Other Sponsorships each have their own page again (November 21, 2026); every record opens inside its company’s card.
Logging Emails: mismosponsor@mismo.org
  • Forward an email to mismosponsor@mismo.org, or Cc or Bcc it on an email to a sponsor, and it lands on that sponsor or prospect’s record under Emails, the same way 360@ works for Member 360.
  • The portal finds the company from the outside address in the email (for a forward, the person in the forwarded email): first by a contact email it already has, then by the company’s email domain.
  • If several companies share the address, it waits on the Pipeline under Emails to mismosponsor@ for someone to pick the right one, or mark it Not Needed.
  • If no company matches and it’s a real company address, a new prospect is made from it and the email is logged there. It’s listed with Looks Right so someone can check it. Free email (Gmail and the like), automated senders and tools never make a prospect.
  • To choose the company yourself, put sponsor: Company Name as the first line when you forward, or [sponsor: Company Name] in the subject.
  • Emails are logged by whoever has the portal open, and every hour by the robot, so nothing waits long. Nothing is ever sent from the mailbox.
Other Sponsorships and Sponsorship Types
  • Any sponsorship that isn’t a summit or a workgroup is an Other Sponsorship, kept by type. The AI Webinar Series with MBA is the first type; its records moved here from the old AI Webinars tab unchanged.
  • New Sponsorship… → A New Sponsorship Type on the Pipeline adds a type: its name, what each one is called, what share MISMO keeps, and for a partner series who gets the past-due notice. One-offs are tracked under their type, and the type gets its own button on the Pipeline.
  • A type shared with a partner (the AI Webinar Series is split in half with MBA) shows what MISMO keeps in every figure, and drafts a past-due notice to the partner once an invoice is over net 30. Any other type drafts the invoice email to the sponsor. Nothing is sent until you read it and press Send.
  • Payment arrives from MISMO Money the same way it does everywhere else in this portal — matched on the Aptify order ID.
Limits and Known Gaps
  • MISMO Money payments are recorded when the portal is opened. The recording happens inside the portal. Payments are recorded the next time anyone who can edit opens it, not the moment MISMO Money loads them. If nobody opens the portal, nothing is recorded until someone does. Someone who can only view the portal doesn’t trigger it.
  • Some payments never reach MISMO Money. Aptify payments recorded as an MBA ACH payment or by credit memo aren’t in its payment exports. Kastle AI’s $15,000 was one. Bernie enters the date it was paid on his Registration Desk (or anyone can enter the Payment Received date on the record here), and it is marked Paid.
  • MISMO Money covers recent payments only. It covers payments from 1 January 2025 on. Older payments are already on their records.
  • Payment follow-ups need a click. They don’t send themselves on a timer, because nothing here emails people without someone choosing to send it. The card shows when one is due.
  • Custom packages show one summit publicly. The Sponsorship Requests list holds one row per sponsorship, so for a custom Series it carries the Winter choice. Spring and Fall choices show here on Inventory but not on the public page.
  • The price list is kept in two places. Tiers, prices and opportunities are in the prospectus and in this portal (for Inventory and custom packages). A change to one needs the same change in the other.
  • The prospectus and this portal count availability separately. The prospectus counts the Sponsorship Requests list; Inventory counts this portal’s records. They agree as long as every change reaches the list.
  • Changes to this portal go through its GitHub repository. Each change is reviewed there before it goes live, so don’t edit the live file directly.
Where Things Live
  • This portal’s page: GitHub, GitMISMO/sponsorship.
  • Its records: GitHub, GitMISMO/sponsorship-data (private). That’s applications, prospects, workgroup sponsorships, other sponsorships and their types (settings), the emails logged from mismosponsor@ (mailin, touchpoints and the monthly mailtext files), the email requests and the payments file MISMO Money writes, which the portal only reads. The old AI webinar file (webinars) is kept as it was.
  • The mismosponsor@ mailbox: the Power Automate flow Sponsorship - mismosponsor Mailbox on Jonna’s account adds each email to the mailin file.
  • The prospectus: GitHub, GitMISMO/Sponsorship-Prospectus.
  • SharePoint, MISMO Summits site: the Sponsorship Requests list and the Sponsor Logos library.
  • Power Automate: the intake flow (prospectus to list), the availability flow (list to prospectus) and the Sponsorship Team Emails flow (emails, list updates, Summit HQ).
  • Sign-in and access: MISMO Resources accounts, with Sponsorship access given per person.
  • The full record of decisions: the MISMO Sponsorships project notes (programs/summits/PROGRAM.md).
A series sponsorship is held as one record at the full price. On the Money tab it is split evenly across that year’s three summits, the same way the Summit P&L dashboard splits them. Everywhere else in this portal — the Pipeline, Applications and Inventory — a series deal is counted once, at its full amount.

Import Aptify Payments

The same two exports you give the Financial System. Matched on the Aptify order ID, which this portal holds as the invoice number.

Nothing changes until you press Apply. This never marks a sponsorship unpaid: a payment can be real and still be missing from these files, which is what happened to Kastle AI's $15,000 when it was booked under MBA ACH instead of MISMO ACH.

Import Prospects from a Sheet

The team's prospects spreadsheet (.xlsx), with its Comments sheet. New companies and new comments are added; nothing already in the portal is overwritten.

Nothing changes until you press Add. Statuses are never changed from the sheet, and a field that already has something in it here keeps it.
