A series sponsorship is held as one record at the full price. On the Money tab it is split evenly across that year’s three summits, the same way the Summit P&L dashboard splits them. Everywhere else in this portal — the Pipeline, Applications and Inventory — a series deal is counted once, at its full amount.

Import Aptify Payments

The same two exports you give the Financial System. Matched on the Aptify order ID, which this portal holds as the invoice number.

Nothing changes until you press Apply. This never marks a sponsorship unpaid: a payment can be real and still be missing from these files, which is what happened to Kastle AI's $15,000 when it was booked under MBA ACH instead of MISMO ACH.

Import Prospects from a Sheet

The team's prospects spreadsheet (.xlsx), with its Comments sheet. New companies and new comments are added; nothing already in the portal is overwritten.

Nothing changes until you press Add. Statuses are never changed from the sheet, and a field that already has something in it here keeps it.
